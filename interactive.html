Interactive works

Art that shows the system making it.

These pieces are instruments rather than animations. Each one hands you a control — seed, threshold, aperture, color — so the image becomes a meeting between a rule, your eye and your choices. A print is one state of the system; the page is the system still running.

Accumulation principle · I

Residual Warmth

Seeded streams of translucent color pass again and again over a paper ground. Nothing is erased: each line stays, dims, bends and adds to the whole.

Meaning arrives through sustained attention, not a single gesture.

Accumulation principle · II

Ink Blooms

Drops become clouds of short radial walks. Same substrate as Residual Warmth, but the generator changes from a stream to a site.

Attention doesn’t always travel as a line; sometimes it pools, bleeds and makes its own weather.

Aperture principle

Umwelt

A perceptual-frequency instrument after Uexküll’s “self-world”: the seed fixes the world, and the aperture decides which slice of it you get to see.

The world is not everything that exists; it is what the sensing window lets in.
Open workRead the philosophy

As a whole. ARTTRA treats an artwork as an encounter rather than a fixed object. A still image is an artifact; an interactive work shows its process. You don’t only look at the finished thing — you handle the parameters that decide how it becomes visible.

Lineage. Residual Warmth and Ink Blooms follow the Accumulation Principle: a persistent ground, small additive contributions, and a constraint that keeps them from collapsing. Umwelt follows a neighboring Aperture Principle: a reality larger than any one rendering, a perceptual filter, and a trace of what was left out.

Turn a state into a print

Any state you find can be made into a physical piece — archival paper, canvas, framed or metal — printed to order and shipped worldwide.

  1. Open a work and tune the seed and settings.
  2. Download the PNG, or note the seed and settings.
  3. Email them with the size and format you’d like.
  4. We confirm size, format and price before anything is printed.
